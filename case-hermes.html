Hermes, aself-hosted agent.

My own AI agent on a private server. I talk to it through Telegram, and it keeps my routine running so the details don't slip.

01

The problem

A daily routine full of small, repetitive tasks, and the constant risk of missing an important detail somewhere in between.

I wanted something that kept track of it all for me, and that I could talk to from my phone.

02

What I built

  1. Step 01

    Keep track of the day

    Hermes holds my daily tasks and reminders, and nudges me before anything slips.

  2. Step 02

    Compile what I read

    It gathers news and articles and turns them into a short brief for the day.

  3. Step 03

    Run the routine

    Scheduled jobs run on their own, and I check in or hand it new work through Telegram.

04

The result

A personal assistant that keeps my day on track, and my reading in one place.

05

What I learned

Plenty went wrong at first. It burned through tokens, wasn't optimised, and used the wrong skills, or the wrong agent, for the job.

Most of the work since has been tuning it: matching each task to the right agent and skills, and spending fewer tokens doing it.
