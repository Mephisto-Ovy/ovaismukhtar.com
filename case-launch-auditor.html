Campaign launchauditor.

A tool that checks every planned campaign touchpoint actually went live across twelve markets, and flags SEO gaps before anyone has to ask.

Role
Designed & built it, solo
Built with
Python · Playwright · AI
Year
2026
Status
Version 2 in progress
01

The problem

Every big campaign launches across a dozen markets at once. Each market adapts copy, titles and positions, which is fine, but sometimes a planned banner or offer simply never goes live.

Checking meant opening page after page in every market, the guidebook in another window. Slow, easy to miss something, and always on launch day.

02

What I built

  1. Step 01

    Load the plan

    Screenshots of the campaign guidebook become a checklist of every planned touchpoint.

  2. Step 02

    Scan every market

    A headless browser visits each market's pages like a real visitor, allowing for local copy and layout.

  3. Step 03

    Flag what matters

    Missing touchpoints and SEO issues surface first, with an AI summary. One click to rerun.

03

How it flows

InputGuidebook
EngineScanner
Visits12 markets
OutputReport
04

The result

A launch check that took a day now takes minutes.

05

What I learned

Version one checked pages against the exact plan, and markets kept "failing" for perfectly sensible local changes. Version two asks a simpler question, was each touchpoint activated in some form? That is what the team actually needed to know.
